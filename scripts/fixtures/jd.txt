Junior Data Analyst - Growth Team

We are looking for a Junior Data Analyst to join our growth team. You will build dashboards, analyse customer behaviour, and support the marketing team with data.

Required qualifications:
- 2+ years of professional experience in a data analyst role
- Strong experience with SQL and relational databases
- Experience developing dashboards using React or a similar frontend framework
- Proficiency with Excel, including PivotTables
- Experience with data visualisation tools such as Tableau

Preferred qualifications:
- Experience with Python for data analysis
- Experience with ETL pipelines
- Excellent communication and stakeholder presentation skills
- Experience working in cross-functional teams

Responsibilities:
- Build and maintain dashboards for the marketing team
- Write SQL queries to extract and transform data
- Present findings to stakeholders
- Collaborate with engineering and marketing
